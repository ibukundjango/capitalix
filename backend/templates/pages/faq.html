General Banking

Capitalix is a modern financial services brand offering deposit accounts, credit cards, loans, business banking, and financial planning resources – all backed by digital tools and genuine personal support.

We offer checking, savings, money market, and certificate accounts for personal customers, plus business checking, business savings, and business lending products for companies of all sizes.

Accounts

We offer everyday checking accounts designed for daily transactions, with digital access, no minimum balance requirements, and straightforward terms. Business checking is also available for companies.

Loans
